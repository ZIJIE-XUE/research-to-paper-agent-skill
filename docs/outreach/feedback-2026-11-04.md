# Feedback follow-up: experiment handoffs and claim wording

Source: [Imbad0202's comment on the ARS showcase post](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills/discussions/1047#discussioncomment-19390382), read on 2026-11-04. The account is the owner of the ARS repository; this is a design suggestion, not evidence that the author ran or endorsed this skill.

## Feedback and assessment

The comment recommends explicitly handing over plan-versus-execution differences, negative results and known limitations, and reviewing how claim strength can change across revisions even when files remain consistent. It also identifies the limits of structural checks and points to ARS's [claim-strength guidance](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills/blob/main/shared/references/claim_strength_ladder.md).

Our existing guidance already retained failed experiments and limited claims, but lacked a concrete handoff record and an explicit comparison of old/new claim wording. We accepted a small documentation/template improvement. No runtime bug or semantic detector was claimed or introduced.

## Change in 1.1.2

- Add an unfilled experiment-handoff template with explicit unknown states for deviations, negative outcomes, limitations and claim boundaries.
- Explain how to bind a filled record to an existing checkpoint and material passport without changing the checkpoint schema.
- Add revision guidance for comparing claim scope, certainty and predictive/causal meaning against evidence.
- Preserve the boundary that scripts do not infer claim strength, detect unregistered semantic changes or prove data authenticity.

The material is independently written; the ARS reference is acknowledged, not copied. No community reply was posted automatically. Software tests and the synthetic demo verify compatibility of the existing tools, not the quality of semantic judgments.
